Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Puffaloo Picture Helper ("the app") is a personal tool that connects to your Google Photos account using Google's OAuth sign-in.

What the app accesses

With your permission, the app accesses your Google Photos library only to let you view and organize your own pictures.

How data is used and stored

The app runs on your own computer. Your photos and sign-in tokens stay on your device. We do not collect, store, sell, or share your photos or personal information on any server, and we do not use them for advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
